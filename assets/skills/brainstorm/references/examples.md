# Brainstorm Examples

## Bad
- Emitting a single favored design without evaluating competing archetypes.
- Duplicating question entries in `brainstorm.md` instead of registering them in `artifacts/questions.md`.
- Directly editing source files during an exploratory spike.

## Good
- Write a disposable script to `artifacts/scripts/probe.sh`, test timing, and record the trade-off in `artifacts/brainstorm.md`.
- Register the unresolved choice under `### Brainstorm — <topic>` in `artifacts/questions.md`.
