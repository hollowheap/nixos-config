# Slop Reference

Category defaults, not unconditional bans. The brief earns exceptions; a finding must name the line that earns one. Distilled from Impeccable's rule registry.

## Page scaffolds

- Nested cards: a card inside a card. Flatten to sections; spacing replaces the container.
- Icon tile stack: same-size icon square above every heading. Drop the tile, or put the icon in the heading line.
- Kicker or eyebrow above a heading: delete the label. The heading carries its own weight.
- Hero eyebrow chip: a pill label above the H1. Same failure, more chrome.
- Numbered section labels (`01 / 02 / 03`): keep only when the number carries sequence the reader needs.
- Oversized H1: a display headline that consumes the first viewport. Size from content, not impact.
- Monotonous spacing: one gap everywhere. Group tight, separate generous.
- Edge-flush cards: a card touching the scroll container edge. Give the row padding.
- Same-size icon/heading/text card grid as the page structure. Cards are the lazy container.

## Surfaces

- Side tab: a colored `border-left` or `border-right` above 1px. Use a badge or a label.
- Border accent on a rounded element: pick the border or the radius.
- Hairline border plus wide soft shadow: the ghost card. Declare elevation once.
- Hard offset shadow (`4px 4px 0`): only inside a committed neobrutalist world.
- Glow, radial halo, radial spotlight: decorative light. Keep only when the subject emits light.
- Buried raster: an image under a wash or opacity. Show it or cut it.
- Text occlusion: an overlapping element covers text.
- Decorative glass or blur: keep only as a specific material effect.

## Color and type

- AI palette: indigo/violet on near-black, or default product blue. Derive the palette from the subject.
- Cream palette: beige ground, serif display, terracotta accent. The category costume.
- Overused font: Inter, Poppins, or the platform default as the only voice.
- Flat hierarchy: two sizes and two weights across a whole page. Establish four to five steps.
- Italic serif display: the editorial default. Pick it only for an editorial subject.
- Gradient text: emphasis comes from weight or size.
- Crushed tracking: below -0.04em.
- Wide tracking on body text: fine for labels, wrong for paragraphs.
- All-caps body text: labels only, never prose.
- Justified text on screen: ragged right.
- Tiny body text and undersized functional text.
- Measure beyond 75ch, tight leading, cramped padding, body text at the viewport edge.
- Unbalanced heading: one orphan word on the last line. Constrain the measure.
- Heading crowded against the previous block: more space above than below.

## Media and motion

- Shape-assembled illustration: circles and triangles standing in for a scene.
- Organic clip-path: a polygon approximating a photo edge.
- Decorative grid background or repeating stripes: no canvas, map, or blueprint under them.
- Auto-scrolling marquee.
- Bounce or elastic easing.
- Pulsing status dot and decorative blinking cursor.

## Copy

- Em-dash overuse.
- Marketing buzzwords and aphoristic cadence (`Not X. Y.`).
- Theater framing (`Powered by AI`, `Built for the future`).
- The same text repeated inside one container.

## Runtime and accessibility

- Broken or placeholder image; uncaught script error on load.
- Content invisible at rest: opacity or clipping hides what should be visible.
- Text overflow inside its container; positioned child clipped by an overflow ancestor.
- One column stretching the first viewport.
- Low contrast text; gray text on a colored background.
- Skipped heading level.

## Use

- Report `file:line`, selector, or computed value; severity P0-P3.
- A brief can earn any entry. Name the earning line; otherwise rewrite the element.
- These are patterns, not bans on a technique. SVG that draws geometry is not an illustration.
