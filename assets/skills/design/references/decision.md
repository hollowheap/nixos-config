# Decision Record

Group accepted decisions under the H3 architecture category: `### System`, `### Module`, `### Framework`, `### Data`, or `### Operations`.

### <Category>

- [x] Dxx [Rxx]: <accepted decision>
  - Context: <problem, constraints, and forces>
  - Alternatives: <rejected options and why>
  - Consequences: <what changes, what gets harder>
  - Arch: <section name or none>
  - Resolves: <Qxx or none>
  - Supersedes: none | Dyy
  - Verify: <command or acceptance>

## Rules

- Record accepted decisions only; unresolved choices stay in `questions.md`. An accepted decision may remain `[ ]` until its child specs complete.
- The H3 category owns the classification so reviews can find the choice.
- `Arch` is the exact architecture H2 name, never a path or anchor.
- Cite the decision that it supersedes, if any.
